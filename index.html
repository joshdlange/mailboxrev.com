Straight answers

So what's the catch?**

We were hoping you'd ask.

Who is the contract with?
You. Each program runs on the partner's own agreement, usually signed directly between your venue and the partner. Mailbox Rev is not a middleman holding your money.
What does setup take?
For the carrier Wi-Fi program, about an hour of your IT team's time, done remotely. The TV program adds a small device that a technician or your own IT fits. Other programs vary, and we tell you before you commit.
How does Mailbox Rev get paid?
The partner may pay Mailbox Rev a commission or referral fee when a venue signs up. Our terms cover it in full: How this works.
Does every venue qualify?
No. It depends on your equipment, your location and the partner's agreement. If a program is not a fit, we will say so.
Is there anything new to buy?
For the Wi-Fi and TV programs, no. They run on the network and screens you already have, and the TV device is supplied for qualified venues.
