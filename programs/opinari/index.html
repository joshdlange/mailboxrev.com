FAQ

About Opinari

01What is Opinari?
Ad overlays inside live streams on Twitch, YouTube and Kick.
02Who is it for?
Brands targeting Gen Z, multi-platform advertisers and live content operators.
03How are ads triggered?
By keywords or moments in the stream, or by chat links timed to on-screen moments.
04Do the ads interrupt the stream?
They are built as overlays that sit within the content rather than break into it.
05How is performance measured?
Real-time tracking of impressions, clicks, engagement and conversions, in reports you can share.
06Which platforms does it run on?
Twitch, YouTube and Kick.
07What does it cost?
That depends on the campaign, so we talk it through on a call and never put numbers on a web page.
08How do we start?
Talk to us. We check fit before we make any introduction and say so if it is not right for your brand.
