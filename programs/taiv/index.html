FAQ

About TAIV

01What is TAIV?
An AI-powered smart-TV platform that turns commercial breaks into venue promotions or paid ads.
02What does it cost?
Nothing for qualified venues in the U.S. and Canada. Equipment, installation and support are included.
03How and when do we get paid?
You receive a revenue share based on the ads shown at your location, paid shortly after TAIV is paid by the advertiser.
04How much will we earn?
It depends on how many TVs you have, how long guests stay and what is on. A person walks you through it on a call, and we do not put numbers on a web page.
05What control do we have over what plays?
You choose your own content and schedule, set ad categories, block categories such as competitors, and set times by TV or zone. You can see daily revenue.
06What does installation involve?
A plug-and-play fit by a certified technician, or by your IT team. No technical experience is needed on your side if you use the technician.
07Which venues qualify?
Any business with TVs on site and customers watching may qualify. We check fit first and say so if it is not right.
08What if something breaks?
TAIV provides remote and on-site support, and fixes or replaces its equipment.
