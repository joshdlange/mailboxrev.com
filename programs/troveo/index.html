FAQ

About Troveo

01What is Troveo?
A company that licenses non-public, real-world data for AI. Its business data program covers records from systems such as chat, email, CRM, support, finance and project tools.
02What is the assessment?
Troveo's free tool. Per Troveo, you answer 10 questions in under 5 minutes, with no obligation, and get an estimate, the factors behind it, a ranked list of your systems and a recommended next step.
03What happens to names and private details?
Troveo says names, contact details, credentials and client identities are scrubbed to one documented standard before your data is put to work, and that buyers get anonymized data with a manifest. Those are Troveo's statements and its terms govern. Ask Troveo for the detail.
04Will anyone know the data is ours?
Troveo says your company is never named publicly, and that buyers are introduced confidentially only after an agreement is signed.
05Who owns the data?
Troveo says you do. It licenses the data on your behalf, and ownership never transfers.
06What does it cost, and what does it take?
Troveo says nothing: no fees, no setup costs, no deductions from your payouts. Getting started takes a short scoping call and a few hours of exports, with no integrations and no new software.
07Can we license the same data elsewhere?
Troveo says exclusivity is time-limited, has to be earned, and comes with clear exit terms. Ask for the exact terms before you sign.
08Should we check anything first?
Yes. Business records can include information about your staff and customers, and your own contracts may limit what you can license. Check your customer contracts, and get legal advice if you are unsure. Mailbox Rev does not give legal advice.
09How much will we earn?
It depends on your systems, your team and your history. Troveo's assessment gives an estimate, and we do not put numbers on a web page.
10Who is the agreement with?
You and Troveo, on Troveo's own terms. Mailbox Rev is a Troveo partner and may be paid for referrals.
