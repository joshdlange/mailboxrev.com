FAQ

About SmartTab

01What is SmartTab?
A point-of-sale and payments platform built for bars, nightclubs and restaurants.
02What if the internet drops?
Staff can capture payments offline, and they process when you are back online.
03How do guests pay?
Chip, swipe, tap or Apple Pay to open a tab, and Tap to Pay on mobile.
04Can we manage menus and staff remotely?
Yes. Reporting, menu and pricing changes and staff additions all work from any device.
05Does it handle more than one location?
Yes. It has an enterprise mode with shared management and reporting.
06What support is included?
Onboarding and go-live support, plus remote support at any hour.
07How do we know if it is right for us?
We start with a no-pressure evaluation: capability fit, integration with what you have, what the transition involves and whether the numbers work for you.
