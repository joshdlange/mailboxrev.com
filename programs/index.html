Any type of business can earn some mailbox money.

Each program either brings you new business or adds revenue from what you already have. Open one to see how it works, who it fits and what it asks of you.

  1. 01Carrier Wi-Fi
    Carrier Wi-Fi offload

    Hudson House

    Earn from the Wi-Fi you already have.

    Carrier subscribers connect automatically to a separate layer of your existing Wi-Fi, and the venue earns from that traffic. Your main network stays separate and untouched.

    Good fit for
    • Hotels and extended-stay properties
    • Bars and lounges
    • Restaurants
    • Entertainment venues and event spaces
    • Multi-location retail and hospitality groups
    • Coworking and flex office
    Key distinction

    No new hardware required.

  2. 02AI data
    AI data licensing

    Troveo

    Anonymized business data that teaches AI how people decide.

    Troveo licenses business data to AI labs and startups, and the business shares in the revenue. Troveo says it removes names, contact details, credentials and client identities first.

    Good fit for
    • Businesses with years of email, chat or CRM history
    • Teams that keep records in support, finance or project tools
    • Owners who want to know if their data is licensable
    • Businesses that can spare a few hours for exports
    Key distinction

    A free assessment, no obligation.

    View full program

    Point of sale built for a busy night.

    Good fit for
    • High-volume bars and cocktail lounges
    • Nightclubs and entertainment venues
    • Fast-casual and full-service restaurants
    • Hotel food and beverage
    • Multi-concept hospitality groups
    Key distinction

    TV ad revenue

    Turn the TVs you have into revenue.

    Good fit for
    • Bars and restaurants
    • Sports bars
    • Hotels and lobbies

    • Multi-location businesses
    • Any space with TVs and guests who stay a while
    Key distinction

    In-stream ads for brands
    For brands and advertisers

    Reach Gen Z inside the live stream.

    Good fit for
    • Brands targeting Gen Z
    • Twitch, YouTube and Kick advertisers
    • Live content operators
    • Multi-platform advertisers
    • Brands that want direct attribution
